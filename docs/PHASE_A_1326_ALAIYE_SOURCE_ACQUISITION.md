## Scenario-date historical constraint

TDV's *Alâiye Beyliği* records Alâiye/Alanya as a Turkish polity from the late 13th century to 1471. It states that Alanya passed to Karamanoğlu Mecdüddin Mahmud Bey in 1293 and that thereafter the city and surrounding region were ruled by beys affiliated with the Karamanids under Mamluk suzerainty.

This supports the existence/control context for 1326-04-07, but it does not provide a date-specific boundary polygon.

The independent secondary synthesis inspected in this pass also reports Alâiye coins dated 1321 and 1326 in the name of Mamluk Sultan Nasir Muhammad and places Alâiye within changing Karamanid/Mamluk/Ilkhanid relationships. This is dated political context, not boundary geometry.

## Reconstruction gate

A future reconstruction can proceed only after a traceable source artifact or explicitly documented reconstruction basis is acquired.

Required proof record:

1. source identity and immutable reference;
2. artifact bytes and SHA-256 where digital bytes exist;
3. license/provenance and transformation rights;
4. original historical date/scope;
5. explicit interpretation of political control versus maximum extent;
6. georeferencing/control points and residuals, if applicable;
7. uncertainty/confidence;
8. physical-land/coastline validation;
9. topology validation;
10. independent review decision.

Until those fields are satisfied, `polygon` remains null and `geometryStatus` remains `pending-source-acquisition`.
