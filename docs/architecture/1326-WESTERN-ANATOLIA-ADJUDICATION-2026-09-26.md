# 1326 Western Anatolia Adjudication — 2026-09-26

## Saruhan
TDV describes Saruhan as Manisa-centered, controlling the Gediz valley/plain and extending through Demirci, Borlu, Ilıca, Adala and Kemaliye eastward, then westward through Akhisar, Turgutlu, Manisa, Kemalpaşa/Nif, Menemen and the Foça/Aegean coast.

- Regional core: HIGH
- Exact 1326 coordinate boundary: UNRESOLVED
- Geometry action: CONSTRAINT CORRIDOR ONLY

## Karesi
TDV places Karesi over much of Mysia, including Balıkesir, Bergama and Çanakkale lands. Balıkesir was the original center; after Karesi Bey's death before 1328, Yahşi Bey moved the administrative center to Bergama and Demirhan Bey was placed over Balıkesir. The 1328 Byzantine-Demirhan agreement is post-start evidence.

- Balıkesir/Bergama core: HIGH
- Mysian regional extent: HIGH
- Exact April 1326 outer boundary: UNRESOLVED
- 1328 agreement: POST-START CHRONOLOGY LOCK

## Aydınids
TDV describes the early-14th-century Aydınid polity from the Büyük Menderes through Tire, Ayasuluk and Birgi. Mehmed Bey took Aydın-ili in 1308.

- Regional core: MEDIUM-HIGH
- Exact April 1326 boundary: OPEN
- Geometry action: CONSTRAINT CORRIDOR ONLY

## Germiyan
TDV places Germiyan around Kütahya and identifies Yakub Bey's independent rule as c.1300-1340. Kütahya is the political center. Regional influence extends widely, but this does not create exact 1326 borders.

- Kütahya core: HIGH
- Yakub chronology: HIGH
- Exact interfaces: UNRESOLVED

## Hamid/Teke
TDV records Dündar Bey's Antalya seizure and appointment of his brother Yunus, creating the Teke-ili branch. Dündar asserted independence and minted without the Ilkhanid ruler's name from 1321. Under Demirtaş pressure, Dündar was surrendered and killed in 1326.

- Hamid core: HIGH
- Teke/Antalya branch: HIGH
- 1326 state: DATE-SENSITIVE
- Antalya/Teke ↔ Hamid: RECLASSIFY / BRANCH-CORE
- Exact 1326 boundary: UNRESOLVED

## Karaman/Konya
TDV dates Karaman's capture of Konya, Gevele and Beyşehir to 1328-1329.

Therefore assigning Konya to Karaman in April 1326 solely from later evidence is rejected.
